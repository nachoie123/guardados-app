What it won't do

Your saves are a list of what you care about. Guardados keeps that list yours:

  • No AISearch and folders run on plain keyword rules. You can paste your own Gemini key to refine them; without it, nothing goes to any AI.
  • No accountNothing to sign up for. You log in on instagram.com and tiktok.com themselves, inside the app.
  • No passwordYou type it into Instagram's and TikTok's own pages. The app never sees or stores it.
  • No priceFree and open source, MIT licence.
  • No server that can read itWhat goes to your phone is sealed first. Scroll down.

To your phone,
sealed on the way.

Your Mac Server Your phone
  1. Sealed on your Mac.

    Before anything leaves, the Mac locks it with AES-256-GCM (Apple CryptoKit). Only what changed gets packed.

  2. A box the server can't open.

    A small Cloudflare Worker keeps the sealed packages. It never gets the key, so all it holds is noise.

  3. The key goes by QR.

    Press Conectar el móvil and scan. The key rides in the link's # part, which browsers never send to a server.

  4. Your phone opens it.

    The same library, searchable, at nachosanbenito.com/guardados.

Install on your Mac

About a minute. Step 3 happens only the first time.

  1. Drag it into Applications

    Open Guardados.dmg and drag Guardados onto the Applications folder.

    Guardados
    Applications
  2. Open it. macOS stops it. Click Done.

    macOS says Apple could not verify “Guardados” is free of malware. It says that about every app that isn't notarized by Apple. Click Done, not Move to Trash.

    “Guardados” Not Opened

    Apple could not verify “Guardados” is free of malware that may harm your Mac or compromise your privacy.

    DoneMove to Trash
  3. Open Anyway, once

    Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to Security and click Open Anyway next to “Guardados” was blocked. Confirm with your password or Touch ID. From then on it opens with a double-click.

    Privacy & Security
    Security
    “Guardados” was blocked to protect your Mac.Open Anyway
  4. Then: import

    Log in on the Instagram and TikTok windows and press Importar ahora (import now). The app speaks Spanish for now.

Why the warning? Notarizing an app needs a paid Apple developer account ($99 a year). This is a free side project, so it isn't notarized. The full source code is on GitHub, and you can check that your download is exactly the file I built. In Terminal:

shasum -a 256 ~/Downloads/Guardados.dmg

should print c33ae2824d913ad0f750526294e088dd7bc8c97a4f73bd8757ffc2f3fe6c35f2

Prefer the Terminal for step 3?
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Guardados.app"
