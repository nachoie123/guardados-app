Guardados

Find anything you ever saved.

Your Instagram saves and TikTok favorites in one place, searchable and sorted into folders and a map. No AI, no account, free.

Download for Mac

On your phone

nachosanbenito.com/guardados

In the Mac app press Conectar el móvil (connect phone) and scan its code: your saves arrive end-to-end encrypted.

What it does

Both apps, one list

Imports every Instagram save and TikTok favorite, and keeps going where it left off.

Search like you think

“quick pasta dinner” finds the reel, by caption, title or @creator.

Folders on their own

Recipes, trips, workouts, bars… sorted by keyword rules, in Spanish and English. Move anything by hand.

Every place on a map

Restaurants and spots from the captions, located with Apple Maps.

Encrypted to your phone

AES-256-GCM on your Mac. The key travels in the QR, never to the server.

Yours

You log in on instagram.com and tiktok.com themselves. The app never sees a password; data stays on your Mac.

Install in a minute

  1. Open Guardados.dmg and drag Guardados into Applications.
  2. The first time, macOS says it could not verify “Guardados”. That's normal for indie apps not notarized by Apple. Click Done, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. Only once.
  3. Log in on the Instagram and TikTok windows and press Importar ahora (import now). The app speaks Spanish for now.
